// FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly do you respond to inquiries?
+

We respond to all new inquiries within 4 business hours (9am–7pm IST, Monday–Saturday). For urgent projects, WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us and typically gets a response within 30 minutes.

Do you work with international clients?
+

Yes, we work with clients globally. For international clients, invoicing is available in USD. We're a fully remote team, so timezone differences are handled via async Slack communication and scheduled video calls.

What is the minimum project size?
+

Our sachet/one-off packages start from ₹1,499 (e.g., product photo editing). For ongoing retainers, our Growth plan starts at ₹24,999/month. We believe every business, at any stage, deserves quality digital work.

How does milestone-based billing work?
+

We split projects into clear milestones (e.g., 30% on kickoff, 40% on design approval, 30% on final delivery). You only pay each milestone after reviewing and approving the deliverable. This protects your investment at every step.

Can I see your work before committing?
+

Absolutely. Our portfolio page has 6 detailed case studies. We're also happy to arrange a 30-minute Zoom call where we walk you through relevant past projects, methodologies, and answer any technical questions you have.

Is source code ownership transferred to me?
+

Yes — upon final payment, you receive 100% ownership of all source code, design files, video project files, and content assets. We use private GitHub repositories during development and transfer ownership at project close.
